## Some tips

- For the Author of this “Fast”, Corner is chilling as Raptors come one straight path, less defences and they get distracted by side players, also corner has more space for eco, Fast dislikes high eco corner players normally on “his” corner as they attract a lot raptors additional to his aggro.
- For new players: spec a game or 2 to see how this is played. If you wanna see butts watch “Sunbutt” on BAR replays.
- Visit the Links page of https://rcorex.github.io/nuttyb-config/ for Widgets and other infos.
"Reclaim Selected" - OP one button Reclaim - "Holo Place" - auto skip to next when turret. “Layout Planner” by Noryon helps you to place stuff right.
- Use SPACE+X to see “Bomb Radius” of buildings.
- Mod needs a highend PC, you and you’r teammates will have no fun if you have a potato. Sometimes GFX on lowest helps. “Lowest” also helps your eyes.
- Try to reach 20-30K E with T1 converter only.
- Build turrets only when you’r metal balance is positive.
- When you’r neighbour builds a mini wall you need to connect to it :/
- The map Metal Plate has 25 Wind, Wind has 2x the efficency to Fusions. T1 Wind chain (some dead = all dead).
- More eco means more raptors and also means more Eggs to reclaim. When you want more raptors start a EFUS far away with a single builder.
- Core T3 Shield (Sanctuary) is a bomb, ARM T3 Shield near each other chains and Legion one has much HP.
- ARM com get’s experience points with buildings its made and shares its experience with new buildings.
- T1 Converter cost 1M, they have a bad ratio but are good until around 30K E. T1 Converter take a lot space, you want no more than 20-30K E with them.
- 3 x T1 Turret have the same BP as one T2 but at lesser cost.
- Fast’s start: ~30 T1 Wind, 10 T1 Turret for T2 Wind, 8 T1 Turret for T1 converters. Build more T1 Turret when you have a positive Metal income. Not before! 
- Some defences like Quad Guard are crush save. While T2 Wall isn’t. T1 wall is a paper wall.
- For example Dragon’s Rage and Dragon’s Fury have ~6x DPS on Metal cost to EPIC Bulwark.
- ALT+SHIFT+SPACE splits builders up
- Any con including com can do skip to next in Queue with the „N“ key.
- Base Builder and T3 AIR AIDE Can ressurect.
- When you farm – less is more, for farming use ARM Beamer or Core Twin Guard, some T1 turrets to reclaim and at start drones on repeat reclaim. Keep in mind that hives have very very strong aa.
- As a farmer you can get more metal when you have more hives targeting your stuff.
- Multiple farmers at the same hive steal each other the farm.
- Core Dragon’s Maw or the Legion Flame defence do very very good. Raptors don’t like to get burned.
- At end of grace you need: Wall, 2-3 Shield, 16+ Maw, 3+ Flac or more T1 AA. If you don’t have that and you have high aggro you dead.
- Legion Bastion is great but it does friendly fire, kills walls.
- ARM com build Basilica is better than Legion Olympus, which is better than COR Basilisk which is better than non experienced Basilica.
- Raptors seem to love Converters, you can block them with T2 Wind wall 🙂
- Juno is required at 50% EVO about 8-12 in front of you’r entrance, Fast does them as a line on repeat. Juno kills Kamikaze and Paralyzer - Kamikaze insta kills your defences when it reaches.
- T2 Turret en mass and later T3 do better than T3 straight for eco.
- You want lots, lots and lots of buildpower for Epic Fusions.
- All “Artillery” weapons, small or big are shit. They do low dps and friendly fire.
- Starfall, Ragnarok and Calamity are the best examples for bad weapons.
- Units without shield are very, very weak. Anything that has no shield is in danger.
- As a side player the wall to your next side player is important, the wall to the corner you can lazyly build.
- Front T3 Turrets are very important for reclaiming, put some of these in “repeat” mode and reclaim a area, these won’t do anything else then. Also align theier range with LRPC range.
- Olympus has the lowest range, Basilica the 2nd and Basilisk the highest.
- For me Olympus does best for a long time. But then I do ARM Com Basilica for the damage.
- All com can repeat DGUN (“repeat” then DGUN), Core com can repeat dgun the line of raptors. Legion as well but it has “shotgun” which means it spreads.
- Don’t build on you’r teammates square except you ask them.
- Do not build converters next to fusions, converter boom = efus boom. Space them with 2 rows of T3 turret or 3 rows of T2 Turret.
- The backline is meant for efus, if you build over them you chain your neighbour or he chain you.
- When you kill Penguins fast enough you don’t need eco shield until like 5 min’s before Queens.
- You should have 2-3 spaces for Converters as they chain.
- Not building on frontline kills your neighbour, building to far in front steals your neighbour aggro.
- EPIC Bulwark better than EPIC Pulsar but ARM com (as all exp stuff) best.
- EPIC’s must be closed for beeing an effective wall. You click one then CTRL+X then “On”
- If you can, have 2 separated 2 layer walls. Also plan side walls, so you get a “fort”. Some players hate you for that as others get all queens then.
- T3 Launcher you can get from Cortex Gantry - “Commando”
- When you fill launchers and plan to shoot them. Shoot only half or less than half on your front. Keep the rest for urgent stuff.
- Queens don’t blow when you reclaim them, needs a lot luck and buildpower to get them reclaimed at the right time.
- T3 Shield have higher efficiency per Metal than T2.
