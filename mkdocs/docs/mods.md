# BAR Mods

Beyond All Reason allows you to install mods ! This is awesome because it can make it much easier to play Raptor games.

They are basically `.lua` scripts that you add to you game files, and they can do a lot.

## Where to get them

**RCore**

[NuttyB Configurator](https://rcorex.github.io/nuttyb-config/) - You can go to the `links` tab and take a look at the widgets listed there. Those are especially used in NuttyB Raptor games.

**Official BAR Discord**

[Widget channel](https://discord.com/channels/549281623154229250/1113845509891829810)

[Other-mods channel](https://discord.com/channels/549281623154229250/1129127537763692654)

**Github**

You can probably find some custom mods on Github.

## Disclaimer

Scripts are commands that your computer will run. Be careful of what you download and install.

## How to install

Go to your `Beyond All Reason` installation folder. Copy the `.lua` files you wish to use to the `Widgets/` folder of your game.

> Beyond-All-Reason\data\LuaUI\Widgets

In game, you can see the list of installed widgets by pressing `F11`.
